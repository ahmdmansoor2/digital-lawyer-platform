📜 Kuwait Labor Law No. 6/2010 Key Articles

Article (51) — Gratuity Calculation Basis
The worker is entitled to an end-of-service gratuity of 15 days' remuneration for each year of the first 5 years, and one month's remuneration for each subsequent year, capped at 1.5 years' remuneration (18 months).
Article (53) — Resignation Entitlement Scale
Under open-ended contracts: <3 years of service (0%), 3–5 years (50%), 5–10 years (66.67%), and 10+ years (100% full gratuity). Female employees resigning due to marriage within 1 year receive 100%.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions — Kuwait Labor & Legal Services

How is end of service calculated in Kuwait?
According to Article 51 of Law 6/2010: 15 days' salary per year for the first 5 years, plus 30 days per year for subsequent years, capped at 18 months' salary.
What are the resignation rules under Kuwait Labor Law?
Article 53 provides: <3 yrs (No gratuity), 3-5 yrs (50%), 5-10 yrs (66.67%), 10+ yrs (100%). Female employees who marry receive full gratuity upon resigning within one year.
How to check judicial cases on Kuwait MOJ?
Through moj.gov.kw or Sahel App using the automated case number or Civil ID to track hearing dates, verdicts, and enforcement orders.
Can foreign investors own 100% of a company in Kuwait?
Yes, under Law No. 116 of 2013 through KDIPA, foreign entities can establish 100% foreign-owned companies with tax exemptions of up to 10 years.
