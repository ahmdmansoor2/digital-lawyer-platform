📜 Key Qatar Statutes

Law 14/2004 — Labour Law

• Art. 54: End-of-service = 21 days' basic wage per year for first 5 years + 28 days' basic wage per year thereafter. Total capped at 1 year's wage.

• Art. 61: Employer may terminate with notice (min 1 month) or immediate dismissal for cause per Art. 61.

• Labour Dispute Settlement Committees handle employer-worker disputes after MoL mediation fails.

QFC Companies Regulations

• QFC operates under English-style common law framework with its own courts and regulatory authority.

• 100% foreign ownership permitted · corporate tax at flat 10% on qualifying income.

• Suitable for financial services, consulting, holding companies, and regional HQ operations.

❓ FAQ — Qatar Legal Hub

How is end-of-service calculated in Qatar?
Article 54: 21 days' basic salary per year for first 5 years, then 28 days' basic salary per subsequent year. Total capped at one year's wage. Basic salary only — allowances excluded.
What is the QFC and how does it differ from mainland Qatar?
The Qatar Financial Centre is a business-friendly free zone operating under English-style common law with independent courts. It allows 100% foreign ownership and offers a 10% flat tax rate on qualifying profits, making it ideal for financial services and regional headquarters.
