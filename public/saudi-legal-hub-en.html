Business Setup & Foreign Investment in KSA
New Companies Law (Royal Decree M/132) + MISA framework
Simple Joint-Stock Co.Most flexible startup vehicle

1. Simple Joint Stock Company (SJSC)

  • No minimum capital required by law.
  • Can be founded by a single shareholder (natural or legal person).
  • Multiple share classes: ordinary, preferred, redeemable, convertible.
  • Managed by one or more directors — no board requirement.
Single-Member LLCPersonal asset protection

2. Single-Person LLC (SMLLC)

  • Limited liability shield unless owner commingles assets or acts in bad faith.
  • Instant commercial registration via Saudi Business Center online portal.
  • Suitable for solo entrepreneurs and wholly-owned subsidiaries.
MISA & RHQ Program100% foreign ownership

3. Foreign Investment & Regional HQ License

  • Full foreign ownership permitted across service, industrial & trade sectors.
  • RHQ license: 30-year corporate income tax and WHT exemption for multinationals relocating to Riyadh.
  • Special Economic Zones (SEZ): Ras Al-Khair, Jazan, King Abdullah Economic City, Cloud Computing SEZ.

Key Saudi Statutes & Regulations
Selected provisions with practical application notes
Civil Transactions Law M/191

Contracts & Tortious Liability

• Art. 94: "A contract is the law of the contracting parties."

• Art. 97: Hardship doctrine — court may adjust burdensome obligations from unforeseen general events.

• Art. 120: Fault causing damage to another obliges compensation.

Evidence Law M/43

Digital Evidence Standard

• Art. 29: Electronic evidence carries the same weight as written proof.

• Art. 30: Includes digital records, e-signatures, email correspondence, and documented messaging apps.

• Art. 66: Witness testimony barred for claims exceeding SAR 100,000 without written proof.

Labour Dispute Pathway

From Qiwa Settlement to Court

Step 1: File via Qiwa amicable settlement (21-day window).

Step 2: If unresolved → referred electronically to Primary Labour Court via Najiz.

Step 3: Appeal within 30 days → Execution Court enforces final judgments.

Commercial Courts Law

Commercial Disputes Jurisdiction

Commercial circuits hear all disputes between merchants, company disputes, bankruptcy filings, and IP infringement cases.

Filing is fully electronic through Najiz with mandatory pre-case disclosure of documents and expert appointment where needed.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions — Saudi Legal Hub

How is end-of-service gratuity calculated if I resign?
Under Article 85: less than 2 years = no gratuity. 2–5 years = one-third. 5–10 years = two-thirds. 10+ years = full gratuity. However, resignation under Article 81 (employer breach) grants 100% regardless of tenure.
What is the maximum probation period under Saudi Labour Law?
90 days, extendable once by mutual written agreement to a maximum of 180 days total.
Can foreigners own 100% of a business in Saudi Arabia?
Yes, most sectors allow full foreign ownership after obtaining a MISA investment licence. Certain activities (oil exploration, military, real estate in Mecca/Medina) require special approvals or Saudi partnership.
