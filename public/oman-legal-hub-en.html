📜 Key Omani Statutes

RD 53/2023 — Labour Law

• Art. 38: Notice period — 30 days (monthly wage) / 15 days (others).

• Art. 39: Arbitrary dismissal compensation: min 3 months, max 12 months gross wage + full gratuity.

• Art. 61: Post-service gratuity not less than one basic wage per year of service. Proportional for fractions. Last basic wage is the calculation basis.

RD 52/2023 — Social Protection Law

• Replaces traditional gratuity with a savings system for Omani nationals.

• Art. 61 applies only to workers NOT covered by this law (primarily expatriate workforce).

• Employers may settle pre-existing service rights into the savings system or pay cash.

❓ FAQ — Oman Legal Hub

How does the dual-era gratuity calculation work?
Service before August 2023 is calculated at the old rate: 15 days' basic wage per year for the first 3 years, then one month per subsequent year. Service after that date is calculated under the new law at a minimum of one month's basic wage per year. The calculator above splits automatically based on contract start date.
Does resignation forfeit end-of-service in Oman?
No. Unlike some Gulf states, resignation in Oman does not strip the worker of their earned gratuity. Forfeiture applies only to dismissals under the specific grounds listed in Article 40.
